FAQ

Answers to the main questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing a website so that AI answer engines - ChatGPT, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews - can understand your business and cite it in their answers. SEO targets ranked links in Google; GEO targets being named inside the single answer an AI gives. The core tools are Schema.org structured data, answer-first content and clear entity information (who you are, where you operate, what you offer and what it costs).

How is a SystemFlow website different from a regular web design project?

Most studios deliver a good-looking site. We deliver a site engineered to be found: complete on-page SEO, Schema.org markup (LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList), speakable and answer-first content blocks for AI engines, a fast mobile-first build and an integrated lead form. Optionally, we connect it to an AI assistant that answers and qualifies leads 24/7.

Do I still need a website if I have Instagram or Facebook?

Yes. AI answer engines and Google rely heavily on crawlable pages with structured data, which social profiles do not provide. A website is also an asset you own - social accounts can be restricted or change their algorithms overnight - and it gives your Google Business Profile a stronger destination to point to.

What is Schema.org markup and why does it matter?

Schema.org is the shared vocabulary that Google, Bing and AI systems use to read structured data. In JSON-LD form it tells machines, unambiguously, that your page describes a business, where it is located, which services it offers and which questions it answers. FAQPage markup can earn rich results in Google and gives AI engines clean question-and-answer pairs to cite.

Will my business appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

GEO significantly improves the odds, but nobody can guarantee a placement - AI engines decide independently what to cite. What we can guarantee is a technically correct foundation: valid structured data, crawlable content and clear entity information. Results also depend on domain authority, brand mentions elsewhere on the web and content quality.
